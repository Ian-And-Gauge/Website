01 · about

Security isn't a product. It's a process.

I got into computers the usual way — curiosity about how things break — and it turned into a habit of taking systems apart to see what makes them tick, and what makes them fail.

Most of my time goes into two things: sharpening penetration testing and vulnerability analysis skills, and writing small Python and C++ tools that automate parts of that work so I can spend more time on the interesting problems.

I'm currently working toward a recognized cybersecurity certification, and I keep a running study of common attack vectors and the defenses that actually hold up against them.
